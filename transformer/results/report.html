Original experiment · saved measurements

What is required to reproduce the original experiment?

Recomputing the original experimental measurements requires the original processed market dataset and the corresponding trained encoder checkpoints. The licensed original data cannot be redistributed and is not included. Rendering saved measurements recreates their report; synthetic data produces new measurements, not the original experimental results.

All 15 original checkpoints are included: linear, patch and convolutional tokenizers at seeds 0, 42, 4395, 5040 and 6118. Recomputing the archived five-seed comparison also requires the original licensed corpus.

Prediction of residual targets

Ridge probes predict the part of each financial target left after subtracting a linear indicator model fitted on training data. Inputs to these probes are frozen embeddings. RankIC is the Spearman correlation of predicted and observed ranks. R² compares squared error with prediction by the evaluation target mean; a negative value means larger error. These are probe scores, not trading returns.

Measurement methods

The report preserves recorded point estimates and confidence bounds. It does not recalculate intervals from rounded tables. RankMe and participation ratio are distinct spectrum measures; the nominal embedding dimension is their ceiling. Sample size and concentration affect these measurements. The training archive records best validation summaries, not per-epoch curves.
