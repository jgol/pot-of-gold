EVOLUTIONARY SEARCH

Genetic programming search results

The experiment evaluates whether transformer embeddings improve genetic programming discovery of zero-days-to-expiration (0DTE) options strategies. These measurements compare indicators, embeddings, combined inputs and a permuted-embedding control using the saved strategy outcomes.

1,999 saved front members · 80 condition/seed/fold runs · 882 held-out dates
2022-09-19 to 2026-04-09

Each main condition uses five search seeds and five folds; the control uses one seed and five folds. Each run uses a search budget of 256 individuals and 100 generations. Whole days are grouped into folds, with four folds used for training and one for evaluation. The frozen encoder was trained over part of this evaluation calendar, so held-out GP dates were not necessarily unseen during encoder training.

Eligibility requires held-out trading activity and a finite training mean. These descriptive counts are separate from the search's champion acceptance tests. Definitions and reproduction

  • Maximum drawdown is the largest decline from a cumulative P&L peak to a later trough. It is not a percentage return without a specified capital base.
  • 5% conditional value at risk (CVaR) is the nonnegative loss magnitude from the mean of the worst 5% of daily outcomes, rounding the number of observations up. Smaller values indicate less tail loss.
  • Annual Sharpe is the daily mean P&L divided by its sample standard deviation, scaled by the square root of 252. PSR(0) and PSR(1) compare that estimate with annual Sharpe benchmarks of zero and one. PSR assumes independent, identically distributed observations and uses an asymptotic approximation with skewness and kurtosis adjustments; it is not a calibrated probability of future profitability.
  • PBO measures how often the strategy selected on one half of a date-block split ranks at or below the median on its complementary half. Here it assesses selection within saved fronts, rather than the complete adaptive search.
  • Saved strategy-level outcomes reproduce these measurements without the licensed minute data. Rerunning the original search or replaying strategies requires the corresponding original data and fitted inputs.
  • The archived training P&L arrays lack date keys, and fitted embedding readout coefficients are not present in the strategy records.
